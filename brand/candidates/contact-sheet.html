iforge brand mark candidates

Three concepts. Exactly one ships, and everything downstream is blocked on the choice: six generated asset files, the manifest theme colour, the newly wired SVG favicon and three in-app logo sites, one of which is a public unauthenticated share page.

Judge at 16px first. It is the binding constraint. Every other size forgives more than it does.

  1. Does it still read as itself in the 16px column?
  2. Does it hold on the dark ground as well as the light one?
  3. Does it survive with the colour taken away, in the neutral rows?
  4. Does anything cross the 409px safe circle in the maskable panel?

Every swatch references the candidate file by relative path; nothing here is an inlined copy, so this sheet cannot drift from the sources. The recoloured rows use the same file as a CSS alpha mask, which is what "one file inverts" means in practice.

A. Monogram

A lowercase i standing on a base bar, knocked out of a solid rounded plate. The plate carries the mass, so what survives at 16px is a filled tile with a letter cut into it rather than a thin letter floating on nothing.

B. Geometric

An anvil cut down to two solid masses: a face tapering left into a horn, and a body flaring to a foot. No letterform anywhere. The waisted anvil was drawn first and rejected because at 16px a thin waist between two bars reads as a serif capital I.

THE THREE REAL IN-APP RENDER BOXES / 36px sidebar, 40px redeem page, 28px public share header

28px box
36px box
40px box

Those three boxes are SQUARE in the code today (size-9, size-10, size-7). A horizontal wordmark dropped into a square box is what you are looking at: at 36px wide the x-height is about 6px. Choosing this option means the three call sites need wider boxes, and plan 03.3-13 is currently written to leave those boxes untouched.

C2. Companion monogram (icon half of the pairing)

A squared F for forge in the same construction as the wordmark. The wordmark already carries the i, and a lone lowercase i at 16px is a bar and a dot that says nothing, so the icon half takes the forge initial instead.
